1The Basics

Filipino Mahjong is played with 108 suited tiles and a set of flower tiles. Four players sit around the table.

Your goal is to build a winning hand of 17 tiles:

5 báhay (sets of three) + 1 pair (the "eye").

A báhay can be a pung (three of a kind) or a chow (three consecutive tiles of the same suit).

Each player starts with 16 tiles; the dealer gets 17 and discards first. On your turn you draw a tile and discard one — unless you can call a set or declare a win.

2The Tiles

There are three suits, each numbered 1–9, with four copies of every tile.

FLOWER TILES

Flowers (including winds, dragons, and seasons) are bonus tiles. They're never part of your 17-tile playing hand — when you draw one, it's set aside and auto-replaced by another tile from the wall.

3Calls — Pung, Chow, Kang

When another player discards a tile you can use, you can call it to complete a set. Calls lock those tiles face-up on the table; they're no longer concealed.

PUNG

Claim any player's discard when you already hold two matching tiles. Forms a set of three identical tiles.

CHOW

Claim a discard only from the player before you to complete a run of three consecutive tiles in the same suit.

KANG

Claim a discard when you already hold three matching tiles. Forms a set of four, and you draw a replacement tile from the wall.

Priority when two players want the same discard, highest first: Tódas (win) › Kang › Pung › Chow.

4Special Moves

SECRET KANG

If you draw a 4th tile matching three already in your hand, you can declare it secretly. All four tiles are placed face-down, concealed from everyone.

SAGÁSA

If you draw a tile matching one of your open pungs, you can extend that pung into a kang, then draw a replacement.

JOKER

At the start of every hand, one random suited tile is chosen as the joker. All four copies of it become wild — each can stand in for any tile when completing your hand.

Jokers are for building your own hand. They can't be used to call pung, chow, or kang from a discard.

BISAKLAT — WON ON THE DEAL

The dealer is dealt a complete winning hand (a full Tódas shape) on the very first deal — no draws, no discards. Breathtakingly rare, and the biggest win in the game.

🃏 JOKER EYE — THE WILD PAIR

A rare, prized self-draw. You're holding five complete sets plus a single, unpaired lone joker. Because the joker is wild, the very next tile you self-draw pairs with it to become your eye — and you win.

You can be holding other jokers inside your sets; all that matters is that one lone joker is waiting to pair with the tile you draw.

Step 2 — you self-draw almost anything; the wild joker pairs with it to become your eye — and you win.

The eye lone joker + the tile you draw = a winning pair 🎉
